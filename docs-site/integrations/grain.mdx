Grain is an AI meeting recorder that captures and transcribes calls. Connecting Grain to CallVault imports your recordings so they live alongside every other call source.

<Note>
  The Grain connector is currently in **beta**. OAuth is the primary connection method, with a bearer-token fallback while OAuth rolls out fully.
</Note>

## Connecting Grain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations settings">
    In CallVault, go to **Settings → Integrations → Grain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect with OAuth">
    Click **Connect** and authorize CallVault from your Grain account. If OAuth isn't available yet, paste a Grain bearer token instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your import settings">
    Decide whether to import your existing Grain recording library or only sync new recordings going forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What syncs

For each imported Grain recording:

- **Transcript**
- **Recording title**
- **Date and duration**
- **Attendees** — from Grain's calendar invitee data, when available

## Where synced calls land

New calls from Grain land in your **My Calls** workspace by default. Use [Routing Rules](/automation/routing-rules) to route them automatically.

## Managing the connection

Go to **Settings → Integrations → Grain** to check connection status or disconnect. Disconnecting stops future syncs but does not delete calls already imported.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="OAuth isn't available for my account">
    While Grain is in beta, paste a Grain bearer token instead of using OAuth — the option is on the same connection screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="New Grain recordings aren't syncing">
    Reconnect the account from **Settings → Integrations → Grain** — the bearer token or OAuth grant may have expired.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Prefer not to connect an account?

You can paste a Grain transcript, or a public Grain note link to pull the transcript automatically, without connecting an account — see [Paste a Transcript](/integrations/paste-transcript).
