<Info>
  The Zoom integration is **coming soon**. When it launches, you'll be able to connect your Zoom account and have cloud recordings imported into CallVault automatically.
</Info>

## What's planned

When the Zoom integration is available, you'll be able to:

- **Connect your Zoom account** via OAuth — no manual syncing needed
- **Automatically import cloud recordings** after each meeting ends
- **Transcribe and summarize** every Zoom call, just like calls from other sources
- **Route Zoom calls** into the right workspace and folder using [routing rules](/automation/routing-rules)

## Requirements

The Zoom integration will work with Zoom accounts that have **cloud recording enabled** (available on Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans).

## In the meantime

You can still get your Zoom recordings into CallVault today:

1. Download the cloud recording from your Zoom account
2. [Upload the file](/integrations/upload) directly to CallVault
3. CallVault will transcribe and summarize it just like any integrated call

<Tip>
  CallVault supports MP4, M4A, MP3, WAV, and other common formats. See [File Upload](/integrations/upload) for the full list.
</Tip>

## Stay updated

We'll announce the Zoom integration when it's ready. If you have questions, reach out at [support@callvault.ai](mailto:support@callvault.ai).
