When you connect Zoom to CallVault, your cloud recordings flow in automatically after each meeting ends. No downloads, no manual uploads.

<Note>
  This integration works with **Zoom cloud recordings**. Local recordings saved to your computer are not synced automatically — upload those manually via [file upload](/integrations/upload).
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- A Zoom account with **cloud recording enabled** (available on Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans)
- Cloud recording turned on in your Zoom settings: **Zoom Settings → Recording → Cloud recording**

## Connecting Zoom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations settings">
    In CallVault, go to **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect next to Zoom">
    You'll be redirected to Zoom's authorization page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize CallVault">
    Sign in to Zoom (if not already) and click **Allow** to grant CallVault access to your cloud recordings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    You'll be redirected back to CallVault. The Zoom integration should now show as **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What syncs

After a Zoom meeting ends and the cloud recording processes, CallVault will import:

- **Recording** — the cloud recording video/audio file
- **Meeting title** — from your Zoom calendar invite
- **Date and duration**
- **Participants** — Zoom participant list when available

CallVault then transcribes the recording and generates an AI summary.

## Where synced calls land

Zoom recordings land in your **My Calls** workspace by default. To route them automatically to a shared workspace or folder, set up a [Routing Rule](/automation/routing-rules) using meeting title keywords as the condition.

## Sync timing

There's typically a short delay between when your Zoom meeting ends and when the recording appears in CallVault:

1. Zoom processes the cloud recording (a few minutes for most calls)
2. CallVault detects the new recording and imports it (usually within 5 minutes)
3. Transcription runs after import (1–5 minutes depending on call length)

## Managing connection settings

You can check and manage your Zoom connection directly from the **Import** page. When you select Zoom in Import, a collapsible **Connection Settings** section appears at the top of the detail view.

<Note>
  If your Zoom account is not connected, the Connection Settings section opens automatically so you can connect right away.
</Note>

### OAuth connection

The Connection Settings section shows your current OAuth status:

- **Connected** — your Zoom account is authorized and recordings sync automatically
- **Not connected** — click **Connect OAuth** to authorize CallVault with your Zoom account

If your OAuth token expires or you need to re-authorize, click **Reconnect OAuth** from the same section.

### Settings fallback

The **Settings → Integrations** tab still shows all connected sources and their status. Use it for a full overview of your integrations or as an alternative way to manage connections.

### Disconnecting

To disconnect Zoom, click **Disconnect** in the header of the Zoom detail view in Import, or go to **Settings → Integrations → Zoom**. Disconnecting does not delete calls that have already been imported.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My Zoom recordings aren't showing up in CallVault">
    First, verify that cloud recording is enabled in your Zoom account settings. Then open the Zoom detail view in Import and expand **Connection Settings** to confirm your OAuth status shows **Connected**. If it shows as disconnected, click **Connect OAuth** to re-authorize.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Only some meetings are syncing, not all">
    Only meetings recorded to the Zoom cloud are synced. If you record some meetings locally, those won't appear automatically — upload them manually via [file upload](/integrations/upload).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I reconnected Zoom but old recordings aren't importing">
    Zoom only sends new recording notifications going forward. Historical recordings aren't automatically backfilled. You can manually download and upload older recordings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connection Settings shows 'Not connected'">
    Your OAuth authorization may have expired. Click **Connect OAuth** in the Connection Settings section to re-authorize CallVault with your Zoom account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
