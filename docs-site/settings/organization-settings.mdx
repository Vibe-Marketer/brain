Organization settings control everything at the org level — who's a member, what roles they have, what the org is called, and what workspaces exist.

There are two places this lives:

- **Organization** in the sidebar — the currently active organization's own details: name, workspaces, and members
- **Settings → Organizations** — the list of every organization you belong to, letting you switch between them, create a new one, and see workspaces across all of them

You need to be an org owner or admin to make changes.

## Overview

The **Overview** tab (on the **Organization** page) lets you:

- **Rename the organization** — change the display name shown across the app

## Workspaces

The **Workspaces** tab shows every workspace in the organization and lets owners/admins create or manage them.

## Members

The **Members** tab lets you pick a workspace and see/manage who has access to it — the same view as **People → Members**.

### Inviting members

Organization-level invites happen from **People → Pending Invites**. Click **Invite**, enter an email address, and choose a role:

- **Admin** — can manage members and organization settings
- **Member** — can be added to workspaces but has no org-level management access

The organization's creator/owner role isn't assigned through the invite dialog — it belongs to whoever created the organization.

See [Inviting Members](/collaboration/inviting-members) for the full workflow.

### Changing roles or removing members

Manage roles and removals from **People → Members** or **People → Pending Invites**. Changes take effect immediately, and removing someone from the organization removes them from all workspaces without deleting their call uploads.

## Integrations

**Settings → Integrations** shows every connected call source (Fathom, Zoom, Fireflies, Read.ai, Plaud, and more) and its status. Each member manages their own connection; admins get visibility into what's connected.

## Billing

See [Account Settings](/settings/account-settings#billing) — billing currently lives under **Settings → Billing** and applies at the account level.

## Danger zone

Deleting an organization is available from **Organization** or **Settings → Organizations** for owners/admins:

- **Delete organization** — permanently deletes the org, all its workspaces, all calls, and all member associations

<Warning>
  Deleting an organization is irreversible. All calls, transcripts, recordings, AI summaries, and workspace data are permanently deleted. There is no recovery option. Download any important exports before proceeding.
</Warning>
