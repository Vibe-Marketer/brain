The **Organization** page is your central hub for managing everything at the org level — who's a member, what roles they have, what the org is called, and what plan you're on.

Open the Organization page from the sidebar. You need to be an `organization_owner` or `organization_admin` to make changes.

The page has three categories: **Overview**, **Workspaces**, and **Members**.

## Overview

The **Overview** category shows your organization's details and settings in a single view.

### Renaming your organization

If you're an owner or admin, the organization name field is editable directly on the Overview screen. Type a new name and press **Enter** or click **Save** to apply. The updated name appears immediately in the org switcher at the top of the page.

### Organization info

The Overview also displays read-only details about your organization:

- **Type** — Personal or Business
- **Your Role** — your current org-level role
- **Members** — total member count
- **Workspaces** — total workspace count
- **Created** — when the organization was created

### Danger zone

For business organizations, owners see a **Danger Zone** section at the bottom of the Overview. This contains the option to permanently delete the organization and all its workspaces.

<Warning>
  Deleting an organization is irreversible. All calls, transcripts, recordings, AI summaries, and workspace data are permanently deleted. There is no recovery option. Download any important exports before proceeding.
</Warning>

## Workspaces

The **Workspaces** category lists all workspaces in your organization. From here you can create new workspaces and manage existing ones.

## Members

The **Members** category shows everyone who belongs to your organization and their org-level roles.

### Inviting members

Click **Invite member**, enter an email address, and choose a role:

- `organization_owner` — full control including billing and org deletion
- `organization_admin` — manage members and settings, but not billing
- `member` — can be added to workspaces but has no org-level management access

See [Inviting Members](/collaboration/inviting-members) for the full workflow.

### Changing roles

Click the role badge next to any member to change their org-level role. Changes take effect immediately.

### Removing members

Click **⋯ → Remove from organization** next to any member. This removes them from the org and all workspaces. It does not delete their call uploads.

## Teams

Teams are managed from **Collaboration → Teams** — named groups of members that can be added to workspaces together. See [Teams](/collaboration/teams).

## Billing

The **Billing** tab is visible to `organization_owner` only. It shows:

- Your current plan (Free, Starter, Business, Enterprise)
- Seat count and usage
- Next billing date and amount
- A link to update your payment method or change your plan
- Downloadable invoices for past billing periods

To upgrade, click **Upgrade plan** and follow the prompts. Changes to your plan take effect immediately.
