Organization-level configuration is split across two areas in CallVault:

- **People** — manage members, roles, contacts, and pending invites
- **Settings → Admin** — org-wide admin controls (visible to admins only)

## General

To rename your organization or update org-level details:

1. Click your organization name in the top navigation
2. Select **Organization settings** from the dropdown

From here you can:

- **Rename the organization** — change the display name shown in the nav and on invoices
- **Organization URL** — set a custom slug for your org (used in shared URLs)
- **Organization logo** — upload a logo that appears in shared call views and exports

## Members and contacts

Member management has moved to the **People** page in the sidebar. From there you can:

- **People → Contacts** — manage your contact directory
- **People → Members** — view and manage organization members across workspaces
- **People → Pending Invites** — track outstanding invitations and send new ones

See [Inviting Members](/collaboration/inviting-members) for the full workflow.

## Integrations

Integration setup (Fathom, Zoom, YouTube, file upload) has moved to the **Import** page in the sidebar. Each source shows its connection status and configuration options. See the [Integrations overview](/automation/integrations) for details.

## Billing

Billing is now a dedicated tab in **Settings → Billing**. It shows:

- Your current plan (Free, Starter, Business, Enterprise)
- Seat count and usage
- Next billing date and amount
- A link to update your payment method or change your plan
- Downloadable invoices for past billing periods

To upgrade, click **Upgrade plan** and follow the prompts. Changes to your plan take effect immediately.

## Admin

The **Settings → Admin** tab is visible to `organization_owner` and `organization_admin` roles only. It provides system-level administration controls for your organization.

## Danger zone

The **Danger zone** at the bottom of Admin settings contains:

- **Delete organization** — permanently deletes the org, all workspaces, all calls, and all member associations

<Warning>
  Deleting an organization is irreversible. All calls, transcripts, recordings, AI summaries, and workspace data are permanently deleted. There is no recovery option. Download any important exports before proceeding.
</Warning>
