## What counts as an AI action

AI actions include any operation that uses AI processing:

- Generating a call summary
- Creating an AI meta-summary across multiple calls
- Using the re-engagement email generator in contacts
- Smart export with AI meta-summary enabled

Your current usage is visible in **Settings → Billing**, along with how many actions remain in your billing cycle.

## Managing your subscription

Organization owners can manage billing in **Settings → Billing** or **Settings → Workspaces → Billing**:

- View your current plan and next billing date
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Update your payment method
- View invoice history

<Note>
  Only the organization owner can manage billing. If you need to make changes and you're not the owner, ask your org admin.
</Note>

## Upgrading

Click **Upgrade** on any plan card to start the checkout process. CallVault uses Polar for payment processing. After upgrading, new features are available immediately.

Annual billing saves roughly 20% compared to paying monthly.
