## Annual billing

Save with annual billing:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | You save |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $29/mo | $278/yr (~$23/mo) | $70/yr |
| Team | $79/mo | $758/yr (~$63/mo) | $190/yr |

To switch to annual billing, go to **Settings → Billing** and select the annual option.

## What counts as an import

Each call brought into CallVault counts as one import, regardless of source:

- A Fathom recording that syncs automatically = 1 import
- A Zoom recording that syncs = 1 import
- A YouTube video you paste in = 1 import
- A file you upload = 1 import

On the Free plan, you get 10 imports per month. Pro and Team plans have unlimited imports.

## What counts as an AI action

Each AI operation counts as one action:

- Generating a smart title for a call
- Auto-tagging a call
- Generating an AI summary
- Creating content (hooks, posts, emails) from a call
- Regenerating any of the above

See [AI features](/transcripts/ai-features) for more detail on what each AI feature does.

## Managing your subscription

To view or change your plan:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. You'll see your current plan, usage, and billing cycle
3. Click **Upgrade** to move to a higher plan, or **Manage subscription** to update payment details

Plan changes take effect immediately. When upgrading, you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period.

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings → Billing**. After cancellation:

- Your account reverts to the Free plan at the end of the current billing period
- Your calls and data remain accessible
- Features above the Free plan limits become read-only until you re-subscribe

<Note>
  If you are on a Team plan with shared workspaces, cancellation affects all team members. Make sure to coordinate with your team before canceling.
</Note>
