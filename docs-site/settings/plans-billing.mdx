CallVault offers three plans designed for different team sizes and usage levels. All plans include core features like transcription, search, and export — higher tiers unlock more capacity, collaboration features, and AI tools.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0/month | $29/month | $79/month |
| **Annual price** | — | $278/year (save $70) | $758/year (save $190) |
| **Users** | 1 | 1 | 3–10 |
| **Imports per month** | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Workspaces** | 1 | Multiple | Shared workspaces with roles |
| **AI actions per month** | 25 | 1,000 | 5,000 (pooled across team) |
| **AI chat** | — | Minimal | Full |
| **MCP server access** | — | Full | Full |
| **Admin dashboard** | — | — | Included |

## Choosing a plan

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Free" icon="user">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro" icon="rocket">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Team" icon="users">

  </Card>

## What counts as an AI action

An AI action is any time CallVault uses AI processing on your behalf:

- Generating an AI summary for a call
- Auto-tagging a call
- Generating a title
- Creating content (email, social post, blog outline, case study)
- Running a meta-summary across calls
- Generating manager notes

Standard transcription does not count against your AI action quota.

## Upgrading your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Plans & Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Review the available plans and click **Upgrade** on the one you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    You'll be redirected to the checkout page to enter payment details. Once payment is confirmed, your new plan is active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downgrading

You can downgrade to a lower plan at any time from the same settings page. When you downgrade:

- The change takes effect at the end of your current billing period
- Extra workspaces and MCP access become read-only if they exceed the lower plan's limits
- Your call data is preserved — nothing is deleted

## Annual billing

Annual plans save you roughly two months compared to monthly billing. You can switch between monthly and annual billing from **Settings → Plans & Billing**.

## Managing your subscription

All billing is handled through **Settings → Plans & Billing**, where you can:

- View your current plan and usage
- Upgrade or downgrade
- Switch between monthly and annual billing
- Cancel your subscription

<Note>
  If you cancel, your account reverts to the Free plan at the end of the current billing period. Your calls and transcripts are preserved, but you'll be limited to Free tier quotas.
</Note>
