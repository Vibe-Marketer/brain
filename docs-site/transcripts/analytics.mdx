The Analytics dashboard gives you a high-level view of your call activity. Use it to spot trends, understand how time is being spent, and identify patterns across your calls.

To open Analytics, click **Analytics** in the sidebar.

## Overview

The Overview tab shows key metrics for the last 30 days:

- **Total calls** — number of calls in the selected period
- **Total hours** — combined recording time across all calls
- **Average duration** — typical call length in minutes
- **Average talk time** — percentage of time spent speaking (vs. silence or listening)
- **Unique speakers** — number of distinct participants across all calls

Trend charts for calls per day/week and minutes per day/week help you visualize activity over time.

## Talk time

The Talk Time tab breaks down speaking patterns by role:

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Host | Primary speaker or meeting organizer |
| Closer | Sales representative or presenter |
| Client | External participants |
| Support | Technical or administrative support |
| Other | Unclassified participants |

You can also see talk time ratios across call categories (sales calls, team meetings, client reviews, training sessions, and one-on-ones) to understand how different meeting types compare.

<Tip>
  A healthy sales call typically has the rep speaking 40% or less of the time. Use talk time analytics to coach reps who may be dominating conversations.
</Tip>

## Participation

The Participation tab shows how many people join your calls and how evenly speaking is distributed. This is useful for identifying meetings where engagement is low or where a single person dominates.

## Duration

The Duration tab analyzes how long your calls run, with breakdowns by call type. Use this to identify meetings that consistently run over time or to track whether your calls are getting more efficient.

## Content

The Content tab tracks content generated from your calls — clips, published pieces, and winning hooks. This helps you measure how effectively you are turning call insights into reusable content.

## Tags

The Tags tab shows call distribution across your tag categories in a sortable table. For each tag, you can see:

- **Number of calls** with that tag
- **Total minutes** of recorded content
- **Average duration** per call

Click any column header to sort. This view helps you understand which types of calls make up the bulk of your library.

## Time range

Analytics are calculated for the last 30 days by default. Data updates as new calls are imported and processed.

<Note>
  Analytics require at least one call in your library. If you see an empty state, import or sync some calls first.
</Note>
