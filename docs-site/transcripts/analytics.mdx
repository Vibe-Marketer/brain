CallVault includes built-in analytics that give you a view of your call library's patterns. Use analytics to understand how your team spends time on calls, what topics come up most, and how participation is distributed.

## Opening analytics

Click **Analytics** in the left sidebar. The analytics section is organized into categories that you can browse in the left panel.

## Analytics categories

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Overview" icon="chart-pie">
    High-level metrics across your call library — total calls, total hours, average duration, and call volume trends over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Duration" icon="clock">
    Distribution of call lengths across your library. See how many calls fall into different duration buckets and track whether your calls are getting longer or shorter over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Talk time" icon="comments">
    Breakdown of who talks the most in your calls. Useful for coaching — see the talk-to-listen ratio for individual reps or across your team.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Participation" icon="users">
    Metrics on meeting attendance and engagement. Track how many participants join each call and who is most active across calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content" icon="file-lines">
    Patterns in what your calls cover. See which topics come up most frequently based on AI-extracted key topics and tags.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tags" icon="tags">
    Distribution of tags across your call library. See which tags are applied most and least, and track tag usage over time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Using analytics for coaching

Analytics are particularly useful for managers reviewing team performance:

- **Talk time** shows whether reps are listening enough or dominating conversations
- **Duration** reveals calls that ran too long or were cut short
- **Content** highlights trending topics so you can address common customer concerns
- **Tags** help you understand how calls are being categorized and whether your tagging strategy is working

## Filtering

Analytics respect your current workspace context. If you're in a specific workspace, the analytics show data for that workspace only. Switch workspaces or view all workspaces to change the scope.

<Tip>
  Combine analytics with [routing rules](/automation/routing-rules) to auto-tag calls. This makes tag-based analytics much more useful since tags are applied consistently without manual effort.
</Tip>
