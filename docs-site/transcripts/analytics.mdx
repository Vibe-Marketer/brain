The analytics dashboard gives you a high-level view of your call activity. Use it to spot trends in call volume, understand how time is spent, and track participation patterns across your team.

## Accessing analytics

Click **Analytics** in the sidebar to open the dashboard. The dashboard is organized into six tabs, each focused on a different aspect of your call data.

## Overview

The overview tab shows five key metrics at a glance:

- **Total calls** — number of calls in the selected time period
- **Total duration** — combined length of all calls
- **Average duration** — mean call length
- **Total participants** — unique participants across all calls
- **Calls this week** — volume for the current week

Below the metrics, two trend charts show call volume and duration over time.

## Duration

The duration tab breaks down how long your calls are:

- **Average duration** and **median duration** — side-by-side comparison to show whether a few long calls are skewing the average
- **Duration breakdown** — a chart showing the distribution of call lengths (e.g., how many calls are under 15 minutes vs. over an hour)

## Participation

The participation tab focuses on who joins your calls:

- **Average participants per call** — how many people typically attend
- **Most frequent participants** — people who appear in the most calls
- **Invitee analysis** — breakdown of invited vs. attended for calendar-linked calls

Use a time-range selector at the top to narrow the view to a specific period.

## Talk time

The talk time tab analyzes how speaking time is distributed within calls:

- **Host vs. others split** — what percentage of the call the host speaks vs. participants
- **Role breakdown** — speaking time split across up to five different speaker roles
- **Category bars** — talk time distribution by call category or tag

This is useful for coaching — if one person dominates every call, the data will show it.

## Tags

The tags tab shows a sortable table with four columns:

- **Tag name** — the tag applied to calls
- **Call count** — how many calls have this tag
- **Total duration** — combined length of tagged calls
- **Average duration** — mean length per tagged call

Use this to understand which types of calls (demos, interviews, onboarding) take the most time.

## Content

The content tab tracks clip and content metrics:

- **Total clips** — number of highlighted clips saved across calls
- **Clips per call** — average number of highlights per call
- **Clips library** — a searchable table of all saved clips with their source call, timestamp, and content

<Tip>
  Use analytics alongside [routing rules](/automation/routing-rules) — tag calls automatically, then use the Tags and Content tabs to measure patterns over time.
</Tip>
