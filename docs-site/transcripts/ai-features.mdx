Beyond [AI summaries](/transcripts/ai-summaries), CallVault includes several AI-powered features that process your calls automatically. These features run in the background after transcription completes, so you get results without any manual effort.

## Smart titles

When a call is imported, CallVault generates a descriptive title based on the transcript content. Instead of generic titles like "Team Meeting" or "Recording 2026-03-15," you get titles that reflect what actually happened — for example, "Pivot to Paid Ads — Acme Corp" or "Cracked the Hook Pattern."

Smart titles focus on:
- **Decisions made** during the call
- **Breakthroughs** or new strategies discovered
- **Specific problems** identified
- **Pivots** or changes in direction

For one-on-one calls, the other participant's name or company is appended automatically.

<Tip>
  You can always edit a call's title manually. Smart titles give you a useful starting point, especially when you are importing a large batch of calls.
</Tip>

## Auto-tagging

Every call is automatically classified into one of 15 categories based on its content, participants, and title. Tags help you filter and organize your library without manual work.

The available auto-tag categories are:

| Tag | Description |
|---|---|
| TEAM | Internal team discussions, planning, updates |
| COACH (2+) | Group coaching sessions with multiple participants |
| COACH (1:1) | Individual coaching sessions |
| WEBINAR (2+) | Presentations, workshops, large audiences |
| SALES (1:1) | Individual sales conversations |
| EXTERNAL | Podcasts, communities, collaborations |
| DISCOVERY | Pre-sales, qualification calls, initial consultations |
| ONBOARDING | Platform onboarding calls |
| REFUND | Refund or retention calls |
| FREE | Unpaid community sessions |
| EDUCATION | Calls where you are the learner |
| PRODUCT | Product demos and feature showcases |
| SUPPORT | Customer support and technical assistance |
| REVIEW | Testimonials, interviews, and feedback |
| STRATEGY | Internal mission, vision, and strategic planning |

Auto-tagging considers three signals in order:
1. Your custom tag preferences (title keywords, attendee rules)
2. Historical patterns from similar calls
3. Content analysis of the transcript

<Note>
  Auto-tags can be overridden at any time. Go to the call detail view and change the tag from the tag dropdown.
</Note>

## Content generation

CallVault can transform insights from your calls into ready-to-use content. The content engine works in two stages:

### Insight mining

First, CallVault extracts key insights from your call transcripts — surprising quotes, counterintuitive findings, emotional moments, and concrete results. Each insight is scored for relevance and potential impact.

### Hook and post generation

From those insights, CallVault generates:

- **Social media hooks** — attention-grabbing opening lines optimized for LinkedIn and Twitter, scored by virality potential
- **Full social posts** — 150–300 word posts that expand on a hook with supporting points and a call-to-action
- **Email drafts** — short outreach emails (100–150 words) with curiosity-driven subject lines

This lets you turn a single call into multiple pieces of content without writing from scratch.

## AI actions and limits

AI features consume "AI actions" from your plan's monthly allowance. Each operation (generating a title, auto-tagging a call, creating content) counts as one action.

| Plan | AI actions per month |
|---|---|
| Free | 25 |
| Pro | 1,000 |
| Team | 5,000 (pooled across members) |

You can check your remaining AI actions in **Settings → Account**. When you reach the limit, AI features pause until the next billing cycle.

<Note>
  Import-time processing (smart titles and auto-tags) runs automatically and counts toward your AI action limit. If you are on the Free plan and importing many calls at once, you may exhaust your allowance quickly.
</Note>
