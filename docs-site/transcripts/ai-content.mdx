CallVault can generate content directly from your call transcripts and AI-extracted insights. Instead of manually writing a follow-up email or drafting a LinkedIn post from your notes, let CallVault produce a first draft in seconds.

## Content types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Follow-up email" icon="envelope">
    A professional email with subject line, opening, body, and call-to-action — based on what was discussed in the call. Typically 150-200 words.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Social post" icon="share-nodes">
    A LinkedIn-style post with a hook, value section, engagement question, and relevant hashtags. Designed to be 200-300 words and ready to post.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Blog outline" icon="newspaper">
    An SEO-friendly article outline with a title, 4-6 sections with key points, and a conclusion with a call-to-action. Use it as a starting point for a full blog post.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Case study" icon="trophy">
    A structured case study with headline, challenge, solution, results, and key learnings — pulled from the call's insights.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Generating content from a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the call">
    Navigate to the call you want to generate content from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the content generator">
    Click **Generate content** from the call's action menu or the AI tools section in the call detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the content type">
    Select one of the four content types: email, social post, blog outline, or case study.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the tone">
    Choose a tone that matches your audience:

    - **Professional** — formal and business-appropriate
    - **Casual** — conversational and approachable
    - **Friendly** — warm and personable
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add optional context">
    Optionally specify a target audience or add additional context to guide the generation. For example: "This is for enterprise IT buyers" or "Focus on the pricing discussion."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate**. The content appears within a few seconds. Copy it, edit it, and use it wherever you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for better results

- **Use calls with clear AI summaries.** Content generation works best when the call has well-extracted insights and action items.
- **Add context.** Telling the generator who the audience is produces more targeted content.
- **Edit the output.** Treat the generated content as a first draft. Add your personal voice, specific details, and any context the AI may have missed.

<Note>
  Content generation uses your AI action quota. Each generation counts as one AI action against your plan's monthly limit. See [Plans and billing](/settings/plans-billing) for details on quotas by plan.
</Note>
