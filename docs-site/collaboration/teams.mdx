<Note>
  **Teams have been removed.** CallVault does not currently have a way to group members into named teams for bulk workspace access. If you have an existing link to a Teams page, it will redirect you to the app home — there's no team management screen to land on.
</Note>

## What to use instead

Member and workspace access in CallVault is managed individually from **People** in the sidebar:

- **Contacts** — everyone you've interacted with across your calls
- **Members** — pick a workspace to see and manage who has access to it, and invite people directly to that workspace
- **Pending Invites** — see and manage outstanding organization and workspace invitations

See [Inviting Members](/collaboration/inviting-members) for the full walkthrough of adding someone to your organization and to specific workspaces.

If you regularly add the same group of people to new workspaces, there's currently no bulk/group mechanism — each person is added to each workspace individually. If this is a recurring need for your team, flag it to CallVault support so it can be prioritized.
