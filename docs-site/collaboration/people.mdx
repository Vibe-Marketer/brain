The **People** page gives you a central view of every contact associated with your calls. CallVault automatically extracts participants from imported calls, so you can track relationships, categorize contacts, and get reminders when it's time to follow up.

Open People from the sidebar — it's the second item below **Calls**.

## Contacts

Contacts are people who have appeared as participants in your calls. You can also add contacts manually.

### Viewing your contacts

The contacts list shows each person's name, email, type, number of calls, and when they were last seen in a call. You can:

- **Search** by name or email using the search bar
- **Sort** by any column — click the column header to toggle ascending or descending order
- **Click a contact** to open their detail card on the right

### Contact types

Each contact can be assigned a type to help you segment your network:

| Type | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Active customers or accounts you manage |
| **Customer** | Broader customer relationships |
| **Lead** | Prospects and potential customers |
| **Other** | Anyone who doesn't fit the categories above |

To change a contact's type, open their detail card and select a new type from the dropdown.

### Importing contacts from calls

CallVault can automatically import participants from your calls as contacts. To enable this:

1. Open **People** from the sidebar
2. Toggle **Track all contacts** at the top of the contacts list
3. From this point forward, every call participant is added as a contact automatically

If you prefer to import manually, leave the toggle off and click **Import all** when you want to pull in attendees from existing calls.

## Contact details

Click any contact to open their detail card. From here you can:

- **View call history** — see how many calls you've had and when you last spoke
- **Set their type** — categorize them as Client, Customer, Lead, or Other
- **Add notes** — write free-form notes about the contact and save them for your team
- **Delete the contact** — remove them from your contact list (this doesn't affect any calls)

## Health monitoring

Health monitoring helps you stay on top of relationships by alerting you when you haven't spoken to a contact recently.

### Enabling health tracking

1. Open a contact's detail card
2. Toggle **Health monitoring** on
3. CallVault will track when you last had a call with this person

When a tracked contact hasn't appeared in a call within the threshold period (14 days by default), an alert banner appears on their contact card prompting you to check in.

### Sending a check-in

When a health alert appears, click **Send check-in** to open the re-engagement email composer. CallVault generates a draft email using AI based on the contact's details. You can:

- Edit the subject line and body before sending
- Copy the email to your clipboard
- Open it directly in your email client

## Members and invites

The People page also gives you quick access to your organization's **Members** and **Pending Invites** through the category pane on the left.

- **Members** — view team members across your workspaces and manage workspace-level access
- **Pending Invites** — see outstanding invitations that haven't been accepted yet

For full details on managing members and invitations, see [Inviting members](/collaboration/inviting-members) and [Workspace roles](/collaboration/workspace-roles).

<Tip>
  Use the workspace filter in the Members view to see which members belong to a specific workspace, or click **Add Workspace** to create a new one directly from the People page.
</Tip>
