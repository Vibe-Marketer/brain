CallVault automatically builds a contact list from the people who appear in your calls. Each contact aggregates data across every call they've participated in, giving you a single view of your relationship history.

## How contacts are created

Contacts are created automatically when CallVault processes a call. If a participant's name or email appears in a call's metadata (calendar invitees, participant list, or recording info), CallVault adds them to your contact list or updates their existing record.

You don't need to manually add contacts — they appear as you import calls.

## Contact information

The contacts table shows five columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The contact's display name, pulled from call metadata |
| **Email** | Their email address, if available from calendar or meeting data |
| **Type** | A label categorizing the contact: Client, Customer, Lead, or Other |
| **Calls** | Total number of calls this contact has appeared in |
| **Last seen** | The date of the most recent call with this contact |

Click any contact to see their full history — every call they've participated in, with links to each transcript.

## Contact types

Each contact is assigned a type to help you categorize your relationships:

| Type | Color | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Client** | Blue | Active paying clients |
| **Customer** | Green | Existing customers |
| **Lead** | Amber | Prospective customers or leads |
| **Other** | Gray | Internal team members, vendors, or uncategorized |

You can change a contact's type at any time.

## Health alerts

CallVault monitors how recently you've been in touch with each contact. If more than 14 days pass without a call involving a contact, a health alert appears — an amber warning banner with a **Send check-in** button.

This helps you catch relationships that might be going cold before it's too late.

## Re-engagement emails

When a health alert appears, you can generate a follow-up email directly from CallVault:

1. Click **Send check-in** on the health alert banner
2. CallVault opens an email composer with an AI-generated draft based on your recent call history with that contact
3. Edit the subject and body as needed
4. Click **Copy to clipboard** or **Open in email client** to send it

The AI draft references topics from your recent conversations so the email feels personal and relevant, not generic.

<Tip>
  Contacts are most useful when combined with consistent call imports. Connect [Fathom](/integrations/fathom) or [Zoom](/integrations/zoom) so every call automatically updates your contact records.
</Tip>
