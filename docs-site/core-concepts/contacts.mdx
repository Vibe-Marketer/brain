**Contacts** are the people who show up in your calls. CallVault automatically builds a contact list from participant information in your imported recordings — names, emails, and any metadata provided by your call source.

You can find all contacts on the **People** page under the **Contacts** category.

## What you see for each contact

Each contact record shows:

- **Name** and **email** (when available from the call source)
- **Number of calls** they appear in
- **Last seen** date — the most recent call involving that contact
- **Organization** the contact belongs to

## Contact folders

Contact folders let you group contacts by any criteria you choose — role, company, deal stage, or anything else. They live in the sidebar under **Contacts** on the People page and work independently from call folders in your workspaces.

### Creating a contact folder

1. Go to the **People** page
2. Select **Contacts** in the sidebar
3. Click **New Folder** below your existing folders
4. Type a name and press **Enter**

The folder appears immediately in the sidebar. Press **Escape** to cancel.

### Assigning contacts to a folder

Select a contact from the contacts table, then assign it to one or more folders. A single contact can belong to multiple folders.

### Viewing contacts in a folder

Click any folder in the sidebar to filter the contacts table to only the contacts in that folder. Click **Contacts** again (the parent item) to return to the full list.

### Deleting a contact folder

Delete a contact folder to remove the grouping. The contacts themselves are not deleted — they return to the unorganized contact list.

## How teams use contact folders

Contact folders are flexible. Here are some common ways to organize contacts:

| Use case | Example folders |
|---|---|
| By company | `Acme Corp`, `Globex`, `Initech` |
| By deal stage | `Discovery`, `Negotiation`, `Closed-Won` |
| By role | `Decision Makers`, `Champions`, `End Users` |
| By team owner | `Jordan's Accounts`, `Priya's Accounts` |
| By engagement | `Active`, `At Risk`, `Churned` |

<Tip>
  Contact folders and call folders serve different purposes. Call folders organize recordings inside a workspace. Contact folders organize people across your entire organization.
</Tip>
