<objective>
Implement the DB-centralized in-app status updates for in-app reporters (RSP-01: received / in-progress) and the escalation-not-silence status (RSP-03), all hard-gated on `source='in_app_user'` per D-00 and emitted as customer-safe reassurance per D-03. Centralizing in one Postgres trigger on `ticket_events` means the D-00 gate lives in exactly one place that fails closed for every status writer (admin, service-role, autopilot) — avoiding the anti-pattern of duplicating the gate in every caller.

Purpose: One fail-closed gate, one outbox, no leak of operator/internal copy to customers. RSP-01 (received + in_progress) and RSP-03 (escalated) are both lifecycle-event-driven and share the gate, so they belong together.
Output: A trigger migration + a real-DB integration test proving the source gate and idempotency.
</objective>
