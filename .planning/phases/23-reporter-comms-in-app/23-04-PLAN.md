<objective>
Implement the verified-stable resolution summary (RSP-02): on a SHA-matched verified-stable deploy, post an auto-generated plain-English resolution summary in-app — gated on `source='in_app_user'` (D-00) and passed through the default-deny filter from plan 03 (D-02). This plan wires the customer comm into exactly that branch.

Purpose: Customers see "resolved" only when production actually serves the fix, and never see internal evidence. This is the autopilot half of RSP-02 (the filter half is plan 03).

</objective>
