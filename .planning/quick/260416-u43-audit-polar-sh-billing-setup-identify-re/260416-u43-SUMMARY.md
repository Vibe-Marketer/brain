## Findings Summary

### Critical Blockers (billing broken right now)

1. **BLOCKER-01 — Product ID mismatch (P0):** `PlanCards.tsx`, `BillingTab.tsx`, and `MCPTab.tsx` all pass symbolic names (`"pro-monthly"`, `"team-monthly"`, etc.) to `polar-checkout`. Polar's API requires UUIDs. Every checkout attempt fails. The `src/constants/billing.ts` file called for in POLAR-UPDATE-LOG.md Section 9 was never created. The 4 UUIDs are: Pro Monthly `30020903-fa8f-4534-9cf1-6e9fba26584c`, Pro Annual `9ff62255-446c-41fe-a84d-c04aed23725c`, Team Monthly `88f3f07e-afa3-4cb1-ac9d-d2429a1ce1b7`, Team Annual `6a1bcf14-86b4-4ec9-bcbe-660bb714b19f`.

2. **BLOCKER-02 — Success URL mismatch (P1):** `polar-checkout` redirects to `${baseUrl}/settings?tab=billing` but `Settings.tsx` uses `useParams({ category })` routing (`/settings/:category`). The `?tab=billing` query param is ignored. After checkout, users land on Settings with no category selected.

3. **BLOCKER-03 — Trial auto-assigned vs opt-in spec (P2):** `handle_new_user()` trigger immediately starts a 14-day Pro trial on signup. PRICING-TIERS.md Section 10 explicitly requires opt-in. Phase 17 verification accepted the auto-assign, so this may be a deliberate deviation — but it's undocumented.
