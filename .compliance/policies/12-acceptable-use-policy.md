# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

Defines the acceptable use of CallVault systems and customer data by the workforce.

## 2. Scope

All workforce members (principals, employees, contractors) with logical access to CallVault production systems or customer data.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Workforce members may access CallVault systems only for legitimate business purposes related to:

- Building, operating, supporting, and maintaining the CallVault service
- Responding to customer inquiries and support requests
- Operating 7x Systems LLC business functions

## 4. Prohibited Use

The following are prohibited:

- Accessing customer data except as required for support, debugging, or operations, and only with documented business justification
- Copying customer transcripts, recordings, or contacts outside of CallVault systems or workforce-authorized devices
- Sharing production credentials by any channel other than 1Password
- Using CallVault systems to host or distribute personal projects, malware, or non-business content
- Bypassing or attempting to bypass any security control (MFA, RLS, branch protection)
- Performing security testing against the production environment without written authorization from the Information Security Officer

## 5. Acceptable Use of AI Tools

CallVault uses AI subprocessors (Anthropic, OpenAI via OpenRouter) to deliver AI-tier MCP tools. The workforce:

- Must not submit customer transcripts to external AI tools outside the documented subprocessor chain (e.g., pasting customer transcripts into a personal ChatGPT session is prohibited)
- May use AI coding assistants (Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot) for non-customer-data tasks
- Should report any uncertainty about whether a use case is acceptable to the Information Security Officer before proceeding

## 6. Personal Use

Limited personal use of workforce devices is permitted provided it does not compromise the security of CallVault systems or customer data, consume disproportionate resources, or conflict with the principal's business duties.

## 7. Reporting

Suspected violations of this Policy or any other security concern should be reported to `support@callvaultai.com` (which currently routes to the principal).

## 8. Enforcement

Violations may result in revocation of access and, where applicable, termination of engagement under the HR Security Policy.

## 9. Review

Annual or on material change.
