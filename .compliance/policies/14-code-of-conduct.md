# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

Establishes the conduct standard expected of every 7x Systems LLC workforce member.

## 2. Scope

All workforce members — principals, employees, contractors — and any third party acting on behalf of 7x Systems LLC.

## 3. Standards

Every workforce member commits to:

- **Honesty.** No misrepresentation of CallVault's security posture, certifications, technical capabilities, or business state to customers, prospects, regulators, or auditors.
- **Customer respect.** Customer data is handled per the Data Classification Policy and the Data Retention & Deletion Policy at all times. No unauthorized access, no curiosity browsing of customer transcripts.
- **Confidentiality.** Information about customers, prospects, security incidents, and unreleased product work is kept confidential until publicly disclosed.
- **Compliance with law.** Compliance with all applicable laws and regulations including privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA), employment laws, and intellectual property laws.
- **Compliance with policy.** Compliance with this Policy and all other CallVault security and operational policies.
- **No conflicts of interest** that could compromise the workforce member's judgment about CallVault's interests. Material conflicts are disclosed to the Information Security Officer in writing.

## 4. Anti-Discrimination

7x Systems LLC does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law. This applies to recruiting, hiring, compensation, and all other terms of engagement.

## 5. Anti-Harassment

Harassment in any form is prohibited. Reports go to `support@callvaultai.com` (single-principal company; reports involving the principal route to external counsel — to be designated as the workforce grows).

## 6. Reporting Violations

Suspected violations of this Code, any other policy, or applicable law are reported to `support@callvaultai.com`. Good-faith reports are protected from retaliation.

## 7. Acknowledgment

Each workforce member acknowledges this Code at engagement start and at each annual policy review.

## 8. Review

Annual or on material change.
